NFL Line Ledger

NFL betting markets tested with free and cheap data: 4,096 games of closing lines, about 69,000 player props across 35 books and exchanges, and 1.25M Kalshi trades. Everything was re-checked on seasons it wasn't built on.

Bottom line · game lines

What survived

Held up
Moderate wind (10–19 mph) → under
Unders hit ~57% outdoors. Books cut the total by less than a point in 10–19 mph wind, while games actually land 2–3 points lower. It still holds after correcting for multiple tests (q = 0.025), in both halves of the sample, and in 12 of 15 seasons. At 20+ mph, books adjust more and the edge goes away.
—under, 10–19 mph
Held up
Wong teaser dogs (+1.5 / +2.5 → +7.5 / +8.5)
Legs won 76.1% in 2011–19 and 77.6% in 2020–25. A 2-team 6-point teaser at −120 needs 73.9% per leg to break even, or 75.2% at −130. Wong favorites (−7.5 / −8.5) came in at 72.8%, below break-even.
76.8%n = 596 legs
Suggestive
Small home favorites (−0.5 to −2.5) underperform
Home favorites of under a field goal covered 45.7%, so the road dog covered 54.3%. It was on the same side in both eras (55.2% / 53.2%), but the sample isn't big enough to be conclusive (p = 0.07). This fits the known fact that home-field advantage has shrunk, from about 2.5 to about 1.5 points in the closing lines.

Avoid
Moneyline longshots (implied under 15%)
Won 7.8% of the time against 12.2% implied, for a −38% ROI. This is the favorite–longshot bias. Big favorites on the moneyline were roughly break-even.

Noise
Situational ATS angles ("off a bye", "after a blowout loss", "winless team"…)
Of 13,181 spread combos, fewer came up "significant" than chance alone would produce. When outcomes were randomly shuffled, more setups looked robust than in the real data. The top 100 setups from 2011–19 kept their direction only 44 times in 2020–25, and their average edge went to zero.

Where it stands · all rounds

What's left standing

Constraint: bets can only go on Kalshi, Polymarket, Novig or ProphetX, not US sportsbooks. Everything below was checked on seasons or games that weren't used to build it.

Paper trading
Resting orders on Kalshi touchdown-scorer markets
I analyzed 1.25M real pre-game trades (349M contracts, 317 games, standard errors clustered by game). The side resting in the book, rather than taking the price, earned +10.4% on 2+ TD (SE 1.5), +18.7% on anytime TD (SE 5.9) and +4.3% on first TD (SE 2.0). Retail traders buy touchdown "Yes" lottery tickets at the ask, and whoever rests on the other side collects. Game-winner markets were about break-even. It's now running as a paper market-maker on GitHub Actions for 2026, with fills behind the real queue.
+10 to +19%maker return, TD markets
Small, consistent
Prop model at exchange prices
About 90 pre-game features. Feature selection happens inside the training years only, and each season is scored blind. The model's accuracy doesn't beat the market's own price, but its strongest picks (edge ≥ 6%) returned +2.2% at Novig/ProphetX (7,149 bets, SE 1.2), positive in 2024, 2025 and 2026.
+2.2%7,149 bets
Mechanism
Wind forecasts, bet early
When the forecast 4 days out shows 12+ mph wind, books lower the total by 0.9 points before kickoff (calm games: +0.1), and unders went 59%. Archived forecasts only go back to 2024 (69 games), so the win rate is within noise, but the line movement makes betting the under early on Kalshi or Novig the right way to play it.
−0.9 ptstotal move to close
No edge
Taking prices on exchanges and prediction markets
Kalshi game markets are well calibrated. Contracts at 4.5¢ hit 4.5% of the time, and buying "No" on longshots at the ask loses 1–4%. Betting exchanges when they're off the sportsbook consensus lost 5%. When Novig disagrees with the books, Novig is usually right (−11.6% on its "+EV" quotes). Futures: essentially one season, dominated by 2025 favorites collapsing.

Round 3 · player props vs sportsbooks · 2023–2026

Where the props edge is (if you can bet the books)

About 69,000 props from The Odds API, pulled 4 days out, 24 hours out and 10 minutes before kickoff across 35 books and exchanges, then graded against nflverse results. Two grading bugs were caught along the way. Tackles were undercounted against official totals, which created a fake +11% under edge. And players with zero catches or carries were dropped from the "longest" markets instead of being graded as unders.

Strongest lead
Bet US books when they're off the exchange price
Treat the de-vigged Novig/ProphetX price as fair value, and bet regulated US books (DraftKings, BetMGM, Hard Rock, ESPN BET…) whenever the book's price beats it by 2%+. Almost all quotes were under 2 minutes old, so these were bettable prices, not stale data. The profit is nearly all on unders () and mostly on receptions. It's driven mainly by 2025, so it needs this season's live results before any real money goes on it.
—
Structural
Books shade overs
Across all core markets, overs hit while the de-vigged prices implied . Blindly betting unders at the best available price went from a loss in 2023 to about break-even in 2025–26. That's not a strategy on its own, but it's why the +EV bets above are nearly all unders.
—overs hit
Doesn't work
Naive projections (last-8-game average vs line)
Books beat a rolling average easily. Receiving-yard edges lost 4–8%. The only positive pockets are rushing unders, which is just the over-shading above.
−4 to −10%most rules
Small
Getting in early
43% of prop lines move between 4 days out and kickoff. But even knowing in advance which way every line would move, betting the opener in that direction returned only . Openers are barely less accurate than closers.
—perfect hindsight

Things the market already knows

Instead of brute-forcing filter combos, these test specific ideas: power-rating models, luck regression, overreaction, backup QBs and price structure. Models were fit on 2011–19 and scored on 2020–25.

Read before betting any of this

Caveats

  • Closing ≠ what you'll get. These are consensus closing lines. To bet wind unders you have to act on the forecast before the total moves, and the close already includes some of that move.
  • Wind is recorded at kickoff, not from the forecast, so real-world results will be a little worse than this backtest.
  • Teaser pricing varies a lot. Many books now charge −130 to −140 for 2-team 6-point teasers or reprice teasers through key numbers. At −140 the break-even is 76.4% per leg, which is about the whole edge.
  • Samples are small. A 55% true edge takes around 1,000 bets to confirm. Most setups here have 100–300.
  • Structural shifts. Kickoff moved to the 35 (2011) and the PAT to the 15 (2015). The 2020 no-fans season and the 17-game schedule (2021) also changed scoring and home-field advantage.
